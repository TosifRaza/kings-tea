# King's Tea packet mockups

These 50 SVG illustrations were drawn in code for this catalog. They are original vector artwork, not AI-generated images and not photographs of manufactured retail packaging.

Each product gallery pairs its individual packet illustration with four existing licensed tea photographs relevant to its category. Those four category photographs are shared among products in the same category. Photo creators, source pages, and license references are recorded in `../ATTRIBUTION.md` and in product media metadata.

Replace a packet illustration with final package photography when the physical product and packaging are confirmed.
